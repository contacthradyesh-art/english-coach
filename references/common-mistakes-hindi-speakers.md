# Common English Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

Use these as teaching examples, not as a reason to shame the learner. Some expressions may occur in Indian English; offer a widely natural alternative where helpful.

| # | Common mistake | Better English | Quick note |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | I am go to school. | I am going to school. | Present continuous needs -ing. |
| 2 | He go to work. | He goes to work. | Add -s with he/she/it in simple present. |
| 3 | She don't know. | She doesn't know. | Use doesn't with she. |
| 4 | I did not went. | I did not go. | After did, use base verb. |
| 5 | I have seen him yesterday. | I saw him yesterday. | Use simple past with a finished past time. |
| 6 | I am living here since 2020. | I have been living here since 2020. | Use present perfect continuous for an ongoing duration. |
| 7 | I will call you yesterday. | I called you yesterday. | Keep the tense consistent with the time. |
| 8 | I am having a car. | I have a car. | Use have for possession. |
| 9 | Discuss about this. | Discuss this. | Discuss does not need “about”. |
| 10 | Return back the book. | Return the book. | “Back” is usually redundant. |
| 11 | Repeat again. | Repeat that. | “Again” is often redundant. |
| 12 | Explain me this. | Explain this to me. | Explain something to someone. |
| 13 | Tell to me. | Tell me. | Tell takes the person directly. |
| 14 | Say me the answer. | Tell me the answer. | Use tell for giving information to a person. |
| 15 | I am good in English. | I am good at English. | Good at a subject or skill. |
| 16 | Married with her. | Married to her. | The usual preposition is to. |
| 17 | Depend of you. | Depend on you. | Depend on. |
| 18 | Listen music. | Listen to music. | Listen is followed by to. |
| 19 | Wait me. | Wait for me. | Wait for someone. |
| 20 | Enter into the room. | Enter the room. | Enter usually takes a direct object. |
| 21 | He is my cousin brother. | He is my cousin. | Cousin is enough in standard English. |
| 22 | One of my friend. | One of my friends. | One of + plural noun. |
| 23 | She is an university student. | She is a university student. | “University” begins with a /y/ consonant sound. |
| 24 | He is best player. | He is the best player. | Superlatives usually take “the”. |
| 25 | I have a good news. | I have good news. | News is uncountable. |
| 26 | Informations are useful. | Information is useful. | Information is uncountable. |
| 27 | Myself Rahul. | I am Rahul. / My name is Rahul. | Use a complete introduction. |
| 28 | What is your good name? | What is your name? | The shorter question is natural internationally. |
| 29 | I passed out in 2024. | I graduated in 2024. | “Passed out” can mean fainted in many varieties of English. |
| 30 | Please prepone the meeting. | Please move the meeting earlier. | “Prepone” is used in Indian English; this alternative is clearer internationally. |

## Teaching approach
- Pick only the 1–3 mistakes that matter most in the learner's current message.
- Show a corrected example and ask the learner to make one new sentence.
- Explain the pattern in simple Hinglish.
