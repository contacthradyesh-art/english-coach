# English Roleplay Scenarios

Use one scenario at a time. Let the learner speak first after the opening line. Correct up to three important issues, then continue naturally.

## 1. Introducing yourself
**Start:** “Hello! Could you tell me a little about yourself?”
**Goal:** Name, education, interests, strengths, and a confident closing.

## 2. Job interview
**Start:** “Why do you want to work with our school?”
**Goal:** Give clear, relevant answers; practise experience, strengths, and examples.

## 3. At a shop
**Start:** “Good afternoon. How can I help you?”
**Goal:** Ask prices, compare products, request a size, and pay politely.

## 4. Ordering food
**Start:** “Welcome! Are you ready to order?”
**Goal:** Order politely, ask about ingredients, and request the bill.

## 5. Asking for directions
**Start:** “Hello. Can I help you find something?”
**Goal:** Ask where a place is and understand simple directions.

## 6. Talking to a friend
**Start:** “Hey! How was your day?”
**Goal:** Describe daily activities, feelings, and plans.

## 7. Office conversation
**Start:** “Good morning. Have you finished the report?”
**Goal:** Give updates, ask for clarification, and discuss deadlines.

## 8. Phone call
**Start:** “Hello, this is the reception desk. How may I help you?”
**Goal:** Introduce yourself, ask for a person, and leave a message.

## 9. Talking about studies
**Start:** “What are you studying these days?”
**Goal:** Explain subjects, goals, challenges, and study routine.

## 10. Travel and hotel
**Start:** “Good evening. Do you have a reservation?”
**Goal:** Book a room, ask about facilities, and explain a problem politely.

## Difficulty levels
- **Beginner:** short sentences and familiar vocabulary.
- **Intermediate:** add reasons, details, and follow-up questions.
- **Advanced:** practise natural phrasing, polite disagreement, and unexpected questions.
